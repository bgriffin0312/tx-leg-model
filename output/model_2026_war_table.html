2026 TX Legislature — Candidate WAR (Wins Above Replacement)

WAR measures how much a candidate outperformed their district's fundamentals (partisan lean, national environment, incumbency, finance).
+3.0 = won 3pp more than a generic party candidate would in that seat.
Covers 2018, 2022, and 2024 elections (only cycles with candidate name data).
Career WAR = time-decay weighted sum (2024 × 1.0, 2022 × 0.6, 2018 × 0.36).
